📖 How to Connect Real Incoming Indian Calls

        
      


      

        
How It Works: Unanswered Call Forwarding

        

          You do not need to transfer your SIM or give callers a new number. You use standard GSM
          conditional call forwarding. When you are busy, reject a call, or don't answer within 20 seconds,
          your telecom provider (Jio, Airtel, Vi) forwards the call to the AI Assistant.
        


        
Step 1: Get a Virtual Inbound Number

        

          Use an Indian telephony provider adapter such as Exotel or Twilio India.
          They provide an Indian virtual DID number (e.g., +91 80 4712 3456) with webhook capability.
        


        
Step 2: Configure Conditional Forwarding on Your Mobile

        

          *61*<VirtualNumber>*11*25#
        

        

          Dial this GSM MMI code on your phone dialer. *61* sets forwarding when unanswered after 25
          seconds. To cancel anytime, simply dial ##61#.
        


        
Step 3: Point Webhook to Server

        

          Set the provider webhook URL to: https://your-domain.com/api/telephony/webhook. The backend will
          serve Hindi prompts and gather caller speech.
        


        
Costs During Development

        

          Currently, the local demo is 100% free using the built-in
          SimulatedTelephonyAdapter and local NLP. Only configure real telephony once you are ready for
          production.
